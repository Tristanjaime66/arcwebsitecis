Raspberry Lemon Cake

by Lemking

An easy to bake delight made up of tangy lemons and sweet raspberries, sandwiched between a deliciously fluffy and moist cake. Perfect treat to serve during any holiday/gathering.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 and 1/2 cups granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup light brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon lemon zest
  • 1/2 teaspoon pure lemon extract
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 and 1/2 cups cake flour
  • 1 and 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 and 1/2 cups fresh or frozen raspberries
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup whole milk

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350° F (175° C).
  2. Mix together the butter, sugar, brown sugar, lemon zest, lemon extract, and vanilla extract in a large bowl. Add eggs in mixture 1 at a time throughout.
  3. In another bowl, whisk together the cake flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Mix in wet ingredients slowly, add milk and lemon juice.
  4. In another bowl, toss the raspberries with 2 tbsp of flour, then add them to the mixed batter.
  5. Bake in oven for 35 to 40 minutes or until golden brown.
  6. Allow cake to completely cool for 10 minutes before separating the edges and cutting.
  7. Enjoy!
