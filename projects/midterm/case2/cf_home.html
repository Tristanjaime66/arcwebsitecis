Art Contest

Event goers will have the opportunity to show off their artistic skills at our events art contest. 1st place winner will be given convention-exclusive swag and a 10% discount on their next ticket to comic-con!

Panels

Starting at 3:00PM on Fridays and Saturday, and 4:00 PM on Sunday, the guest actors/actresses of this event will be holding a panel where fans can ask them questions or make requests.

Autographs

Nearing the end of the guest panels, fans will be able to obtain autographs from their favorite actors. The cost of the autographs vary, and is left up to whoever is signing.

Come meet actors of many beloved comic/video game characters!

Chris Evans - Robert Downey Jr - Keith David - Scarlet Johansson - Ron Perlman - Tara Strong -John Dimaggio - Laura Bailey - Zoe Saldana - Anthony Mackie - Robert Pattinson - Margot Robbie - Todd Haberkorn - Bryce Papenbrook - Tress MacNeille - Erika Harlacher - Tom Hiddleston - Haley Joel Osment -
& Others!

Artist Alley

Many artists will be setting up booths to sell their merch inside of the exhibit hall. The hall will be open the for entire weekend from 11:00AM to 5:00PM.

Click here to view the list of artists attending, and where their booth will be stationed.

Feeling Hungry?

There will be several food stands/trucks open in the courtyard just outside of the exhibit hall, as well a spacious shaded seating area to rest at.
